CruitA voice-first career companion.

Try Cruit ↗Skip the story ↓
  1. 01 · Customer problem

    Most people don’t know what AI can do for their career, and a wall of text doesn’t help.

    Cruit puts those use cases in front of them, AI first where it helps most and blended in where it doesn’t, with tools you can click and a document taking shape while you talk.

    650+ researched articles bring in 5,000 to 10,000 search impressions a day.

    A router picks one of 10 intents and hands off to a specialist agent.

    The agents run on Agent Pulse, my open-source framework. Tools are schema-validated and plan-gated, replies stream, and an evaluation harness replays real conversations. SvelteKit on Vercel, with GCP Cloud Functions, Firestore, Stripe, GA and PostHog.

02 / AI & IMAGINATION · CASE STUDY

KukulalaA little idea. A whole storybook.

Find it on the App Store ↗Skip the story ↓
  1. 01 · Customer problem

    My kid loves to read and to create his own books, but the current options fell flat.

    Flat narration, art styles you had to know by name, the same names and plots, short page limits and one language. Kukulala is named after my kid’s favourite made-up word.

    TYPICAL NARRATION
    KUKULALA
  2. 02 · Use case

    A one-of-a-kind book for any child, any moment, anywhere.

    Ages 2 to 18, with words and plots that grow with the child. Kids speak their own story, and parents use it for fears, big questions and places worth knowing.

    AGE 3

    Pip was scared of the dark. Then the stars said hello.

    AGE 8

    When the fire alarm shrieked, Maya covered her ears, then remembered the plan she’d practised.

    AGE 14

    Across the salt flats of the Rann of Kutch, flamingos rose in a pink haze as Arjun followed the wild ass tracks toward the horizon.

  3. 03 · Prioritization

    Voice-first, visual book creation came first.

    Then style, then characters, then everything that makes waiting and reading feel good. A few things wait until they can be done well.

    BUILT, IN ORDER
    1 · Voice capture2 · Visual style picker3 · Characters4 · Workbench for consistency5 · Streaming book, page turns6 · Pricing, launch, UI refresh7 · Library, languages, onboarding
    NOT YET
    Book sharingPrinted booksAndroid appWebsite
  4. 04 · UX & design

    It feels like a real book.

    Pages turn with texture, 30+ art styles are picked by sight, and 30+ expressive voices slow down for younger ears, in 60 languages. A little self-playing game keeps kids company while the book is made.

    ▶
    More read-alouds on YouTube ↗
  5. 05 · Growth & onboarding

    Try it before you pay.

    A free 10-page book in onboarding, no sign-in wall, and a public library of example books. Reach comes from Google Ads (about 0.3% click-through so far), Instagram, TikTok, and a YouTube channel of read-alouds generated automatically with HyperFrames.

    Google Ads · YouTube read-alouds · Instagram · TikTokFree 10-page book, no sign-inPublic library of example booksStarter or Adventure plan
  6. 06 · Architecture

    A book that streams, and a job that always finishes.

    Gemini streams the story. The cover is drawn first and becomes the reference for every page, so characters stay consistent. Each stage is tracked and retryable, a failed book refunds its credit, and a child’s photo never leaves the device. A workbench compares prompts, models and costs across languages and styles.

    On-device speech → Groq WhisperRetryable jobsAuto-refund on failurePhotos stay on device
  7. 07 · Try it

    Find it on the App Store.

    The first 10-page book is free.

03 / HOME & HARDWARE · CASE STUDY

Android TV LauncherA calmer, faster home for our TV.

Explore the code ↗Skip the story ↓
  1. 01 · Customer problem

    Our Sony BRAVIA took 10 to 15 seconds to respond, and every click on the remote lagged.

    Meanwhile, the home screen was busy with promotions, sponsored rows and usage reporting. I put the TV in developer mode and, with Claude Code over ADB, found what was running.

    TURNED OFF · STILL INSTALLED
    Sony promotional contentSony Select promoted-apps rowStore demo mode · 3 appsSony usage reportingStock launcher customisations · 2 appsPlay GamesStock launcher and its sponsored rows · 2 apps

    11 apps off. One button brings every one back.

  2. 02 · Use case

    We brought our TV into the AI age, and now it’s actually helpful: a planning tool for the household.

    • What’s the weather now, and in two hours?
    • Is that fact right?
    • What should we cook tonight?
    • How long is the drive this weekend?

    We used to ask Alexa and got answers we couldn’t see. Now we ask the TV.

  3. 03 · Prioritization

    Use cases first, everything else off.

    Speed and privacy came first, then the four things we do every day. We use five apps at most, so the shelf holds five.

    BUILT, IN ORDER
    1 · Debloat the BRAVIA, safely2 · New home screen design3 · Keys sent from the computer4 · Weather, radar, routes, Ask AI5 · Voice from the remote’s mic6 · Ask AI as a chat7 · NASA’s picture of the day
    LEFT OUT
    Trending picksA wall of appsCrypto pricesLG and Samsung
  4. 04 · UX & design

    Glanceable from the couch, over a new photo of space every day.

    Hold the mic button built into the remote and just talk: ask AI, plan a route, find a recipe or check the news. Big type and frosted glass keep the weather, radar and time readable from the couch. The background cycles through NASA’s last five pictures of the day, skipping video days. I love astronomy, and the ⓘ panel lets my kid learn what we’re looking at.

  5. 05 · Setup & safety

    Easy to switch back to the native launcher with one click.

    Nothing is uninstalled. Choosing Original restores Sony’s home screen and every app that was turned off. If the new home screen ever crashes three times in five minutes, it switches back by itself. Setting it up is one sentence to Claude Code.

    Home ScreenFree My TVOriginal

    One click restores Sony’s home screen and all 11 apps

    3 crashes in 5 minutes → switches back on its own

    > Install it on my TV at 192.168.1.50. I live in Toronto.

  6. 06 · Architecture

    A 2 MB app, drawn by hand, that uses the TV’s own ADB.

    Plain Java with no Gradle or libraries, drawn on one Canvas and allocating nothing while it draws. It connects to the TV’s debugging port from inside, so nothing listens on the network. Gemini always runs the latest Flash model, grounded in Google Search for current events. Keys are pushed once and encrypted with Android Keystore.

04 / WRITING · LOCAL-FIRST AI · CASE STUDY

ReefnoteA writing room for novelists.

Explore the code ↗Skip the story ↓
  1. 01 · Customer problem

    Bringing my childhood imagination to life as a hard sci-fi series.

    As a kid I filled notebooks with sketches and stories, including the names of almost 1,000 robots. I want to turn that into an immersive universe. Readable literature should be crafted by a person, so I wanted AI to help me build it from the top down, not write it for me.

    One prompt can’t write a novel worth reading.

    Premise · a sentenceOutline · one pageCharacters · world & rules · factionsChaptersScenesBeats
  2. 02 · Use case

    Two ways to write a story: top down by chapter, or one subplot at a time.

    Plan from the premise down to chapters, scenes and beats. Or, when the book carries several storylines, write each subplot start to finish and assemble the chapters later, instead of switching threads inside every chapter. Keeping the subplots connected stays the author’s job.

    Top down: premise → beatsOr subplot by subplotChapters assembled later
  3. 03 · Prioritization

    Each feature came from a problem I hit while writing.

    Cards were suggested after every message, so I made them on request. The AI sounded like AI, so I added a style guide. My prologue carried several storylines, so I built subplot mode.

    BUILT, IN ORDER
    1 · Top-down cards, links, your own key2 · Cards made on request3 · Chat scope: card, links or free4 · Style guide: book, chapter, scene5 · New layout, pinned chats6 · Subplot mode7 · Export, chat archive, call log, search
    LEFT OUT ON PURPOSE
    One-prompt novelsAI writing straight into the bookAccounts and a cloud server
  4. 04 · UX & design

    The card in the middle, the co-writer on the right.

    The first layout put the card on top and the chat underneath. I rebuilt it so the card reads easily in the centre. Pin a chapter’s chat and keep it open while moving through its scenes. Each card can start a new chat, and older ones are archived.

    After · Before, inset

  5. 05 · Setup & openness

    Open source from day one. Bring your own key, and it all runs locally.

    Clone it, start it, add a provider and key in Settings. Two models do different jobs: one brainstorms and writes, the other turns a chat into card suggestions. Every book lives in one SQLite file on your machine, with no accounts.

    > git clone github.com/mehere14/reefnote
    > npm install && npm start

    Writing modelbrainstorm · draft
    Card modelchat → card suggestions

    Many books · one SQLite file · works offline

  6. 06 · Architecture

    A graph of linked cards, and context retrieved from it for every AI call.

    • Cards link to each other, so the book is a graph.
    • Chat on a card and the AI gets that card, the cards linked to it and its parent. Cards two links away come in as titles only.
    • Questions about the whole book use SQLite full-text search.
    • When it won’t all fit, less important cards shrink to a short summary, then to a title.
    • The AI drafts first. A second pass turns the draft into suggestions I accept or reject.
    • It runs on my Mac, and I reach it from my phone over Tailscale.
    SQLite + full-text searchTwo-pass AI, Zod-validatedEvery change versionedExact context loggedPrivate over Tailscale
  7. 07 · Code

    Still evolving, and open source under MIT.

    I’m using it to write a hard sci-fi series from those childhood notebooks I used to sketch and write in.
